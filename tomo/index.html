Tomo · first look

Built from Pablo's sketch: a robot with a split face, half friendly and half clear acrylic over a circuit board. It has a drawer in its chest for notes, ring-shaped hands and feet that fit together like puzzle pieces.

2D · ink

2D, hand-drawn

Wobbly ink line on paper, matching the Dear Tomorrow style. One warm accent: the glowing eye.

3D · toondrag to turn

3D, toon-shaded

Same design built from simple shapes. The acrylic half is see-through, so you can look into the circuit board from any angle.

What I added to the sketch

  • A warm eye. The round eye glows amber, a small light inside the machine. It blinks.
  • The drawer is memory. Notes go in, and it slides open when Tomo remembers something. Try the button.
  • A living circuit. Tiny lights blink behind the acrylic, so you can see it thinking.
  • Paper and ink palette. Warm cream body, ink lines, pale blue acrylic, green board. The amber eye is the only bright color.
