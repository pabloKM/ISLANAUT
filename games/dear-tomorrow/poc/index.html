Dear Tomorrow: three ways to draw the same morning

A proof of concept. The same test island and the same Waker (your drawing) shown three ways. Drag the slider to see how each style handles the light from dawn to night.

Time of day Morning
1 · 2D inkMy pick

2D hand-drawn

Your drawing used as-is, on an island drawn in wobbly ink that gently "boils" like hand animation.

  • Keeps your line exactly as drawn
  • Lightest on phones, quickest to build
  • Fixed camera; needs front, side and back drawings
2 · 2.5D paper cutout

2.5D paper cutout

A 3D island with your flat drawing standing in it like a paper figure, as in Paper Mario.

  • Real depth and a camera that can drift
  • Still your drawing, same art needs as 2D
  • Line on the world is cleaner and less handmade; more build work
3 · Full 3D

Full 3D

The Waker rebuilt from simple 3D shapes with toon shading, turning freely as they walk.

  • Every angle for free, smooth turning
  • Fewer drawings needed from you
  • Your hand-drawn line is lost; looks more like a toy; heaviest on phones
